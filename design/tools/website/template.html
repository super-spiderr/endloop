FAQ

Questions, before the roast.

Does Endloop block apps completely?

No. The roast is a speed bump, not a lock. You can always close it or earn 5 more minutes, and it never blocks Settings, calls, the Play Store or your home screen.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones. It needs Usage Access, permission to show over other apps, and notifications. On some brands you'll also set battery usage to unrestricted so your phone doesn't put Endloop to sleep; the app walks you through it for your brand.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Apple doesn't let apps show a full-screen roast over other apps, so Android comes first.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up at all. Premium is tied to your Google Play account, so you can restore it on another Android phone.

What's Tanglish?

Tamil written in English letters, the way people actually text. Pick it when you set up, or switch any time in Settings.

Can I change my roaster?

Yes. Pick Loop or Lupe when you set up, and switch any time in Settings. Endloop can change its app icon to match your roaster.

Can I share my roasts?

Yes. Share any roast or your weekly card to Instagram Stories or any app, or save it to your gallery. Nothing is shared unless you tap share.
