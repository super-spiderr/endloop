Endloop is a screen-time tool. You choose apps and daily limits; Endloop warns you as you get close and shows a full-screen roast when you reach a limit. It's a self-help tool to nudge your habits. It is not a parental-control, medical or mental-health product, and it doesn't guarantee any result.

2. The roasts

Roasts are meant to be funny and are about your screen time, not about who you are. You pick how harsh they are (Polite to Unhinged), and you can change that at any time in Settings.
