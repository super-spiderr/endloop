Common questions

The roast doesn't show up when I hit my limit

  • Check Display over other apps is on for Endloop (Endloop → Settings → Permission health shows what's missing).
  • Some phones (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, Samsung and others) stop background apps to save battery. Set Endloop's battery usage to Unrestricted / No restrictions, and turn on Autostart if your phone has it.
  • A few apps, like Files, hide pop-ups from other apps. When that happens, Endloop takes you to your home screen and shows the roast there.

I didn't get the 75% or 90% warning

Make sure notifications are allowed for Endloop and the warning types are switched on in Endloop → Settings → Notifications.

Why can't I raise my limit right now?

On purpose. Lowering a limit works immediately; raising it or removing an app starts tomorrow. Otherwise it would be too easy to cheat at 11 pm.

How do "5 more minutes" snoozes work?

You get 2 per app per day. Each one asks you to give a reason, type a sentence and wait a few seconds. Backing out counts as a win.

The Endloop icon disappeared from my home screen

If you changed the app icon, some launchers remove the old shortcut. Endloop is still in your app drawer; drag it back to your home screen.

Premium: restore, cancel or refund

  • Restore: sign in to the same Google account in the Play Store, open Endloop's Premium screen and tap Restore purchase.
  • Cancel a subscription: Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Endloop → Cancel.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy.

How do I delete my data?

Endloop → Settings → Delete all my data. Everything Endloop stored is on your phone, so this (or uninstalling) removes it. More in the Privacy Policy.
