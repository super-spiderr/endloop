Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026 · Endloop for Android · by Super Spider

The short version. Endloop watches the clock, not your content. It reads how long you spend in the apps you choose, so it can warn and roast you. That information stays on your phone. There's no account, no ads and no analytics, and we don't sell or share your data.

Who we are

Endloop is made by Super Spider, an independent developer based in India. "We" and "us" in this policy mean Super Spider. You can reach us at vigneshbalasubramaniyan1409@gmail.com.

Endloop does not read your messages, photos, contacts, location, browsing, what you watch or type inside other apps, or anything else on your screen. It only knows which app is in front and for how long.

Permissions we ask for

  • Usage Access: to measure time spent in the apps you choose.
  • Display over other apps: to show the full-screen roast when you hit a limit.
  • Notifications: for heads-up, last-call, late-night and weekly roast notifications.
  • Run in the background (foreground service), start at boot, and battery optimisation exemption: so the limits keep working after you close the app or restart your phone.

You can turn any of these off in Android Settings. Endloop will tell you what stops working.

What leaves your phone

  • Nothing, by default. Endloop has no account and doesn't send your usage data to us or anyone else.
  • When you choose to share a roast or weekly card, Endloop creates an image and hands it to the app you pick (for example Instagram). What happens next is covered by that app's policy. If you choose "Save", the image goes to your phone's Pictures/Endloop folder.
  • If you buy Premium, the payment is handled by Google Play; we never see your card or bank details. We use RevenueCat to confirm your purchase and restore it on your other devices. For that, RevenueCat receives a random app ID (not your name or email) and your purchase details from Google Play. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. Use Settings → Delete all my data in Endloop to wipe it at any time, or uninstall the app, which removes it too. Endloop keeps the most recent 500 roasts in its history and replaces older ones. Purchase records kept by Google Play and RevenueCat follow their own retention rules.

Your choices and rights

  • See what Endloop can read at any time in Settings → What Endloop can see.
  • Delete everything with Settings → Delete all my data.
  • Withdraw permissions in Android Settings whenever you like.
  • Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and similar laws, you can ask us about your data or raise a concern by emailing vigneshbalasubramaniyan1409@gmail.com. Because we don't hold your usage data on any server, in most cases there is nothing for us to access or delete beyond what the app itself lets you delete.

Children

Endloop is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect data from them. It collects no personal data from anyone to a server in any case.

Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read. Keep your phone locked to protect it.

Changes to this policy

If Endloop starts handling data differently, we'll update this page and the date at the top before that version of the app is released. Big changes will also be mentioned in the app.
